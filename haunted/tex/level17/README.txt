Level 17 texture pack. Each texture: name.jpg (colour, sRGB) + name_n.jpg (tangent-space normal, OpenGL/three.js), 1024x1024, seamless unless noted.
Baked in Blender from src/level17_tex.py (shared generators: src/texlib.py, framework: src/texbake.py).

Where each one goes:
  bell_plate             The Diving Bell (floor): riveted copper-iron bell plate
  kelp_silt              The Kelp Forest (floor): dark silt and kelp holdfasts
  angler_rock            The Anglerfish Lair (floor): black rock with lure-light flecks
  jelly_silt             The Jellyfish Bloom (floor): pale silt glowing faintly blue
  galleon_deck           The Sunken Galleon (floor): waterlogged deck with barnacles
  search_silt            The Searchlight (floor): grey rippled silt
  whirl_silt             The Whirlpool (floor): silt swirled into spirals
  trench_rock            The Trench (floor): cracked blue-black trench rock
  ink_silt               The Ink Cloud (floor): black silt in drifting ink
  bone_seabed            The Leviathan (floor): seabed scattered with old bone
  sunken_tiles           Shadow Step (tutorial room floor): sunken, algae-dark stone tiles
  abyss_wall             walls (wallImg): deep teal rock face
  abyss_copper           trim (trimImg), pipes, portholes: barnacled verdigris copper
  coral                  coral growths
  barnacle_iron          wrecks, anchors, chains: barnacle-crusted rusty iron
