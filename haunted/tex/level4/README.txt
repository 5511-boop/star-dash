Level 4 texture pack. Each texture: name.jpg (colour, sRGB) + name_n.jpg (tangent-space normal, OpenGL/three.js), 1024x1024, seamless unless noted.
Baked in Blender from src/level4_tex.py (shared generators: src/texlib.py, framework: src/texbake.py).

Where each one goes:
  pier_boards            The Pier (floor): salt-grey boards, green slime at the seams
  tide_sand              Tide Pools (floor): pale wet sand with pools and shell grit
  rowboat_planks         The Rowboat (floor): flaking sea-green boat paint
  ship_deck              The Listing Deck (floor): dark deck planks crusted with salt
  eel_rocks              Eel Alley (floor): wet black rock and weed
  bilge_metal            The Bilge (floor): rusted hull plate under oily water
  valve_plates           The Flood Valves (floor): riveted iron, chipped green paint
  fog_shingle            The Fog Banks (floor): grey sand and shingle
  lamp_tread             The Lamp Room (floor): cast-iron tread plate
  jetty_boards           Dash (tutorial room floor): bleached jetty boards
  sea_wall               walls (wallImg): barnacled stone, salt bloom and algae
  verdigris              trim (trimImg), lamp housing, fittings: verdigris copper
  rope                   rope coils, mooring lines
  buoy_stripes           buoys, life rings: faded red and white bands
