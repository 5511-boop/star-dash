Level 18 texture pack. Each texture: name.jpg (colour, sRGB) + name_n.jpg (tangent-space normal, OpenGL/three.js), 1024x1024, seamless unless noted.
Baked in Blender from src/level18_tex.py (shared generators: src/texlib.py, framework: src/texbake.py).

Where each one goes:
  gate_basalt            The Cracked Gate (floor): basalt with thin glowing cracks
  hammer_iron            The Hammer Hall (floor): sooty iron plates
  channel_crust          The Magma Channels (floor): cooled crust over bright magma
  furnace_brick          The Elemental Furnace (floor): scorched fire brick
  keg_boards             The Powder Kegs (floor): sooty boards with black powder spills
  crucible_slag          The Crucible (floor): splattered slag and spilled metal
  obsidian_floor         The Obsidian Maze (floor): glossy black obsidian, purple sheen
  eruption_floor         The Eruption (floor): heavily cracked, lava breaking through
  hearth_stone           The Smith's Anvil (floor): dark stone hearth slabs
  titan_plates           The Forge Titan (floor): huge riveted black iron with glowing seams
  ash_ground             Bombs (tutorial room floor): grey ash ground
  forge_wall             walls (wallImg): charred black rock with ember glints
  hot_iron               trim (trimImg): heat-scaled iron
  lava                   lava pools, molten metal (pair with an emissive tint)
  obsidian               obsidian pillars, props: conchoidal faceted glass
