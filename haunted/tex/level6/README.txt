Level 6 texture pack. Each texture: name.jpg (colour, sRGB) + name_n.jpg (tangent-space normal, OpenGL/three.js), 1024x1024, seamless unless noted.
Baked in Blender from src/level6_tex.py (shared generators: src/texlib.py, framework: src/texbake.py).

Where each one goes:
  stubble_field          The Scarecrow Field (floor): dead, flattened stubble
  bank_grass             The Two Banks (floor): dark wet grass
  bramble_mud            The Bramble Maze (floor): thorny mud and dead leaves
  swamp_moss             The Hand Swamp (floor): moss over mud, faintly glowing fungus
  isle_peat              The Drifting Isles (floor): black peat
  pumpkin_furrows        The Pumpkin Patch (floor): furrowed soil rows
  boardwalk              Throw (tutorial room floor): rotting boardwalk over the bog
  bog_water              bog water: black water with duckweed (for pools)
  bog_hedge              walls (wallImg): dense dark hedge
  moss_wood              trim (trimImg), posts: mossy rotten wood
  burlap                 scarecrows, sacks: burlap
  pumpkin_skin           pumpkins: ribbed orange skin (wrap around)

Realistic bases from Poly Haven (CC0, https://polyhaven.com):
  brown_mud_02 (Rob Tuytel)
  dirt_floor (eye-candy.xyz)
  fabric_pattern_05 (Rob Tuytel)
  grass_ground (Charlotte Baglioni)
  weathered_planks (Dario Barresi, Dimitrios Savva)
