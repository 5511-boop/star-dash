Level 14 texture pack. Each texture: name.jpg (colour, sRGB) + name_n.jpg (tangent-space normal, OpenGL/three.js), 1024x1024, seamless unless noted.
Baked in Blender from src/level14_tex.py (shared generators: src/texlib.py, framework: src/texbake.py).

Where each one goes:
  cloud_steps            The Cloud Steps (floor): pale stone veiled in cloud wisps
  terrace_terracotta     The Twin Terraces (floor): sun-faded terracotta tiles
  lichen_flags           The Windy Terrace (floor): lichen-spotted flagstones
  thorn_grass            The Thorn Maze (floor): overgrown grass with thorny runners
  kite_lawn              The Kite Run (floor): long lawn
  sundial_gravel         The Sundial Beds (floor): raked pale gravel with brass inlay lines
  bramble_moss           The Bramble Wall (floor): moss and fallen brambles
  storm_slate            The Storm Front (floor): wet dark slate
  bridge_planks_pale     The Hanging Gardens (floor): bleached rope-bridge planks
  queen_mosaic           The Storm Queen (floor): sky-blue and white mosaic
  spirit_glass           Spirit Bridge (tutorial room floor): ghostly pale-cyan glass tiles
  garden_wall            walls (wallImg): pale sandstone balustrade stone
  sky_gilt               trim (trimImg), finials: weathered gilt
  topiary                hedges, topiary
  terracotta             planters, urns: terracotta

Realistic bases from Poly Haven (CC0, https://polyhaven.com):
