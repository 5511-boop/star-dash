Level 10 texture pack. Each texture: name.jpg (colour, sRGB) + name_n.jpg (tangent-space normal, OpenGL/three.js), 1024x1024, seamless unless noted.
Baked in Blender from src/level10_tex.py (shared generators: src/texlib.py, framework: src/texbake.py).

Where each one goes:
  foyer_marble           The Gilded Foyer (floor): white marble with gold veins
  pool_tiles             The Reflecting Pool (floor): wet pale-blue glazed tiles
  prism_glass            The Prism Hall (floor): glass tiles with a faint rainbow sheen
  maze_glass             The Glass Maze (floor): black glass tiles
  crowns_parquet         The Vanity of Crowns (floor): gilded parquet
  shatter_mirror         The Shattering Floor (floor): cracked mirror floor
  rose_marble            The Looking-Glass Gallery (floor): rose marble
  haunted_marble         The Haunted Mirrors (floor): dark grey marble, smudged handprints
  soul_marble            The Soul Exchange (floor): violet marble
  queen_star             The Mirror Queen (floor centrepiece, single image): silver starburst inlay
  silver_tiles           Soul Swap (tutorial room floor): plain silver-grey tiles
  palace_wall            walls (wallImg): lilac silk damask
  palace_gilt            trim (trimImg), frames: bright gilt
  old_mirror             mirrors: old silvered glass with foxing

Realistic bases from Poly Haven (CC0, https://polyhaven.com):
  herringbone_parquet (Jenelle van Heerden, Sergej Majboroda)
