Level 9 texture pack. Each texture: name.jpg (colour, sRGB) + name_n.jpg (tangent-space normal, OpenGL/three.js), 1024x1024, seamless unless noted.
Baked in Blender from src/level9_tex.py (shared generators: src/texlib.py, framework: src/texbake.py).

Where each one goes:
  nave_flags             The Collapsed Nave (floor): flagstones under fallen rubble
  gallery_cobble         The Gargoyle Gallery (floor): worn grey cobbles
  tomb_slabs             The Grapple Relay (floor): tomb slabs with carved inscriptions
  censer_mosaic          The Swinging Censers (floor): faded Byzantine mosaic
  belfry_guano           The Bat Belfry (floor): boards white with guano
  font_wet               The Holy Water Font (floor): wet flagstones and puddles
  reliquary_marble       The Rotating Reliquary (floor): red and grey inlaid marble
  aisle_rubble           The Collapsing Aisle (floor): cracked, dusty broken stone
  statue_dust            The Fallen Statues (floor): pale marble, thick with dust
  king_stone             The Gargoyle King (floor): dark carved stone, stained rust-red
  plain_flags            Grapple (tutorial room floor): plain worn flagstones
  cathedral_wall         walls (wallImg): grey-violet cathedral stone
  wrought_iron           trim (trimImg), railings, lamps: wrought iron
  stained_glass          stained-glass windows: warm leaded glass (glows)
  pew_oak                pews, doors: dark oak
