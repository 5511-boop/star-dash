Level 11 texture pack. Each texture: name.jpg (colour, sRGB) + name_n.jpg (tangent-space normal, OpenGL/three.js), 1024x1024, seamless unless noted.
Baked in Blender from src/level11_tex.py (shared generators: src/texlib.py, framework: src/texbake.py).

Where each one goes:
  reading_parquet        The Reading Room (floor): dark polished herringbone
  catalogue_tiles        The Card Catalogue (floor): small brown and cream encaustic tiles
  lending_boards         The Lending Desk (floor): honey boards worn pale in a path
  forged_carpet          The Forged Shelves (floor): bottle-green carpet
  sealed_cork            The Sealed Booths (floor): speckled cork tiles
  pages_floor            The Turning Pages (floor): parquet strewn with loose pages
  restricted_iron        The Restricted Section (floor): black iron grille
  ink_floor              The Ink Tide (floor): boards drowned in glossy black ink
  vault_steel            The Flickering Vault (floor): riveted steel vault plate
  archivist_rug          The Archivist (floor): deep purple rug with a gold border pattern
  phase_slate            Phase Run (tutorial room floor): grey slate tiles
  archive_wall           walls (wallImg): plum damask above dark panelling
  archive_brass          trim (trimImg), lamps, ladders: dull brass
  bookshelf              bookshelves: rows of book spines
  desk_leather           desks, chairs: green leather
  parchment_ink          scrolls, notices, loose pages: inked parchment
