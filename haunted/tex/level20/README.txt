Level 20 texture pack. Each texture: name.jpg (colour, sRGB) + name_n.jpg (tangent-space normal, OpenGL/three.js), 1024x1024, seamless unless noted.
Baked in Blender from src/level20_tex.py (shared generators: src/texlib.py, framework: src/texbake.py).

Where each one goes:
  return_checker         The Grand Return (floor): the old ballroom checker marble, faded (an echo of Level 1)
  heartbeat_flesh        The Heartbeat (floor): red veined flesh
  trials_parquet         The Gallery of Trials (floor): dark herringbone, old blood in the grain
  breathing_boards       The Breathing Walls (floor): boards giving way to flesh
  banquet_carpet         The Haunted Banquet (floor): wine carpet, spilled wine and crumbs
  letter_tiles           The Letters of HOLLOW (floor): cracked encaustic tiles
  choir_flags            The Ghost Choir (floor): cold chapel flagstones, rimed with frost
  study_oak              The Lord's Study (floor): dark oak boards
  heart_chamber          The Heart's Chamber (floor): glowing red flesh, veins lit from within
  lord_marble            The Manor Lord (floor): black marble with red veins
  soul_slate             Soul Blast (tutorial room floor): plain dark slate
  manor_wall             walls (wallImg): crimson damask, peeling to the plaster
  manor_gold             trim (trimImg), frames, candelabra: old gold
  flesh_wall             the breathing walls, the heart: living flesh
  manor_oak              furniture, panelling: dark oak

Realistic bases from Poly Haven (CC0, https://polyhaven.com):
