Level 13 texture pack. Each texture: name.jpg (colour, sRGB) + name_n.jpg (tangent-space normal, OpenGL/three.js), 1024x1024, seamless unless noted.
Baked in Blender from src/level13_tex.py (shared generators: src/texlib.py, framework: src/texbake.py).

Where each one goes:
  lamphouse_boards       The Lamp House (floor): oily, coal-black boards
  tunnel_rock            The Knocking Tunnels (floor): rough coal-dusted rock
  cart_ballast           The Ore Carts (floor): ballast and spilled coal
  canary_flags           The Canary's Cage room (floor): dusty stone flags
  crystal_rock           The Crystal Cavern (floor): dark rock shot with violet crystal
  echo_wet               The Echo Pit (floor): wet black rock and still pools
  glowworm_rock          The Glow-worm Grotto (floor): damp rock pricked with cyan glow
  warren_earth           The Knocker Warren (floor): freshly dug earth
  collapse_rubble        The Lost Miner (floor): collapsed rubble and splintered props
  knocker_stone          The Old Knocker (floor): dark stone scratched with tally marks
  oil_planks             Lantern Oil (tutorial room floor): oil-stained planks
  coal_seam              walls (wallImg): black coal seam with glinting facets
  mine_timber            trim (trimImg), pit props, beams: dark oily timber
  rail_iron              rails, cart bodies, buckets: rusted iron
  crystal_face           crystals: glowing violet crystal facets

Realistic bases from Poly Haven (CC0, https://polyhaven.com):
