Level 12 texture pack. Each texture: name.jpg (colour, sRGB) + name_n.jpg (tangent-space normal, OpenGL/three.js), 1024x1024, seamless unless noted.
Baked in Blender from src/level12_tex.py (shared generators: src/texlib.py, framework: src/texbake.py).

Where each one goes:
  wrap_checker           The Wrapping Room (floor): faded pink and cream checker
  jack_harlequin         Jack-in-the-Box Alley (floor): harlequin diamonds, red and mustard
  conveyor_rubber        The Conveyor Floor (floor): ribbed black rubber belting
  block_floor            The Alphabet Blocks (floor): painted wooden squares in faded primaries
  tops_rings             The Spinning Tops (floor): concentric ringed tiles
  dollhouse_parquet      The Dollhouse (floor): tiny pink-tinted parquet
  ballpit_mats           The Ball Pit (floor): padded foam mats, faded blue and red
  parade_tin             The Clockwork Parade (floor): teal and brass tin checker
  mouse_boards           The Mouse Maze (floor): gnawed dark boards
  workshop_boards        The Toymaker (floor): workshop boards dusted with sawdust and shavings
  mint_tiles             Decoy (tutorial room floor): pastel mint tiles
  nursery_wall           walls (wallImg): faded striped nursery wallpaper, peeling
  chipped_gold           trim (trimImg): chipped gold paint
  polka_paper            gift boxes, wrapping: faded polka-dot paper
  red_tin                tin toys, drums: chipped red tin
  toy_felt               toy tables, billiard-style felt
