Level 19 texture pack. Each texture: name.jpg (colour, sRGB) + name_n.jpg (tangent-space normal, OpenGL/three.js), 1024x1024, seamless unless noted.
Baked in Blender from src/level19_tex.py (shared generators: src/texlib.py, framework: src/texbake.py).

Where each one goes:
  waking_clouds          The Waking Door (floor): lilac cloud
  hallway_runner         The Endless Hallway (floor): repeating violet runner
  clock_checker          The Twin Clocks (floor): black and lilac checker, slightly melted
  nursery_moons          The Nightmare Nursery (floor): faded rug of moons and stars
  heist_marble           The Dream Heist (floor): pastel marble
  penrose_tiles          The Penrose Stairs (floor): impossible tumbling-block tiles
  shadow_ripple          The Shadow Self (floor): ink-dark floor with silver ripples
  hourglass_sand         The Hourglass (floor): pale lilac sand
  sleep_quilt            The Sleepwalkers (floor): pastel patchwork quilt
  king_obsidian          The Nightmare King (floor): deep purple obsidian with star specks
  rewind_stars           Rewind (tutorial room floor): starfield
  dream_wall             walls (wallImg): lilac wallpaper of moons and stars
  lavender_silver        trim (trimImg): lavender silver
  moon_surface           moons, lamps: pale glowing moon surface
  pillow_cloud           cloud props, pillows
