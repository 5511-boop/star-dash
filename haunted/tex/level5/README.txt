Level 5 texture pack. Each texture: name.jpg (colour, sRGB) + name_n.jpg (tangent-space normal, OpenGL/three.js), 1024x1024, seamless unless noted.
Baked in Blender from src/level5_tex.py (shared generators: src/texlib.py, framework: src/texbake.py).

Where each one goes:
  crevasse_snow          The Crevasse Field (floor): snow split by blue crevasse cracks
  flag_stones            The Prayer Flags (floor): frosted flagstones
  reed_mats              The Meditation Hall (floor): woven reed mats
  lake_snow              The Frozen Lake (shore floor): trampled snow
  lake_ice               The Frozen Lake (the ice itself): dark clear ice, cracks and bubbles
  library_boards         The Silent Library (floor): dark boards, frost creeping in at the edges
  spring_stones          The Hot Springs (floor): wet dark cobbles, snow melted away
  avalanche_snow         The Avalanche Run (floor): rough snow with rock poking through
  wraith_slate           The Wraith Hunt (floor): black slate furred with frost ferns
  boiler_plates          The Boiler Pipes (floor): rusting iron plate, rimed with frost
  glazed_tiles           Lantern Flash (tutorial room floor): blue-grey glazed tiles
  monastery_wall         walls (wallImg): grey-blue stone with flaking white plaster and frost
  frost_silver           trim (trimImg): frosted pewter
  prayer_cloth           prayer flags, banners: faded five-colour cloth
