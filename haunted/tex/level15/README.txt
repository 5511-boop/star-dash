Level 15 texture pack. Each texture: name.jpg (colour, sRGB) + name_n.jpg (tangent-space normal, OpenGL/three.js), 1024x1024, seamless unless noted.
Baked in Blender from src/level15_tex.py (shared generators: src/texlib.py, framework: src/texbake.py).

Where each one goes:
  ante_sandstone         The Antechamber (floor): worn sandstone slabs
  scarab_sand            The Scarab Nest (floor): sand riddled with burrows and beetle shells
  anubis_basalt          The Hall of Anubis (floor): black basalt tiles with gold inlay
  canopic_tiles          The Canopic Shrine (floor): lapis and gold checker
  chute_sand             The Sand Chutes (floor): rippled sand
  blade_stone            The Blade Corridor (floor): cracked stone, old rust-red stains
  sphinx_slabs           The Sphinx's Riddle (floor): huge pale limestone slabs
  granite_floor          The Sarcophagus Hall (floor): dark green granite
  lapis_stars            The Star Shafts (floor): lapis vault with gold stars
  pharaoh_gold           The Pharaoh (floor): gold tiles bordered with glyph bands
  plain_sandstone        Freeze Bolt (tutorial room floor): plain sandstone
  glyph_wall             walls (wallImg): sandstone blocks carved and painted with glyph rows
  tomb_gold              trim (trimImg), sarcophagi, idols: tomb gold
  lapis                  lapis panels, scarab inlays
  tomb_linen             mummy wrappings, linen hangings
