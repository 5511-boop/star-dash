Level 16 texture pack. Each texture: name.jpg (colour, sRGB) + name_n.jpg (tangent-space normal, OpenGL/three.js), 1024x1024, seamless unless noted.
Baked in Blender from src/level16_tex.py (shared generators: src/texlib.py, framework: src/texbake.py).

Where each one goes:
  rope_carpet            The Velvet Ropes (floor): crimson carpet, gold border
  gallery_carpet         The Figure Gallery (floor): dark green gallery carpet
  melting_wax            The Melting Floor (floor): soft cream wax, pooled and dripping
  impostor_marble        The Impostor (floor): large white marble tiles, grey veins
  wax_chess              The Wax Chessboard (floor): cream and oxblood wax squares, softened edges
  wax_bath               The Wax Bath (floor): molten orange wax, swirling (pair with an emissive tint)
  dark_carpet            The Dark Gallery (floor): threadbare black-violet carpet
  exit_tiles             The Fire Exit (floor): scuffed green and cream service tiles
  twin_marble            The Twin Exhibits (floor): rose marble, mirrored veins
  madame_marble          Madame Wax (floor): black marble with gold veins
  vault_tread            Vault (tutorial room floor): dark steel tread plate
  museum_wall            walls (wallImg): maroon striped wallpaper over dark wainscot
  museum_gilt            trim (trimImg), frames, rope posts: gilt
  wax_skin               wax figures: cream wax skin
  rope_velvet            velvet ropes, plinth covers: red velvet

Realistic bases from Poly Haven (CC0, https://polyhaven.com):
