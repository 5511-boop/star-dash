Level 7 texture pack. Each texture: name.jpg (colour, sRGB) + name_n.jpg (tangent-space normal, OpenGL/three.js), 1024x1024, seamless unless noted.
Baked in Blender from src/level7_tex.py (shared generators: src/texlib.py, framework: src/texbake.py).

Where each one goes:
  platform_bricks        Platform 13 (floor): wet, sooty platform bricks
  inspection_carpet      Ticket Inspection (floor): carriage carpet, wine with a gold lozenge
  roof_tin               The Roof Run (floor): corrugated, rusted carriage roof
  yard_gravel            The Switching Yard (floor): oily ballast gravel
  baggage_boards         The Baggage Car (floor): scuffed rough boards
  engine_tread           The Engine Room (floor): sooty tread plate
  dining_parquet         The Dining Car (floor): parquet with old wine stains
  mail_lino              The Mail Car (floor): cracked green linoleum
  coupling_plate         The Coupling (floor): oily coupling plate
  ping_carpet            Ping (tutorial room floor): faded blue carriage carpet
  carriage_panels        walls (wallImg): mahogany carriage panelling
  train_brass            trim (trimImg), luggage racks, lamps: dull brass
  seat_plush             carriage seats: worn green plush
  luggage_leather        trunks, suitcases: scuffed leather
  boiler_iron            the engine boiler, firebox: soot-black iron, heat-blued
